# Calibration: Newton's Second Law

**Question:** Why does doubling the net force double acceleration if mass stays fixed?

**Expected Sage style:**

**Goal:** Connect force, mass, and acceleration using Newton's second law.

**Step 1:** Newton's second law states \(F_{net}=ma\), where \(m\) is mass and
\(a\) is acceleration.

**Step 2:** Solve for acceleration: \(a=F_{net}/m\).

**Step 3:** If mass is fixed, doubling \(F_{net}\) doubles the numerator while
the denominator stays the same, so acceleration doubles.

**Check:** The units are \(N/kg=m/s^2\), which matches acceleration.

**Takeaway:** With constant mass, acceleration is directly proportional to net force.
