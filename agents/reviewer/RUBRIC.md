# Review Rubric

Score each criterion from 1 to 5.
1 = Unacceptable, 2 = Poor, 3 = Acceptable, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent.

## 1. Scientific Accuracy (Weight: 40%)
- **5**: Absolutely flawless. Uses precise terminology. No misconceptions.
- **3**: Mostly accurate, but uses some imprecise language that could lead to minor misunderstandings.
- **1**: Contains fundamental factual errors.

## 2. Pedagogical Effectiveness (Weight: 30%)
- **5**: Brilliant analogies. Perfect scaffolding. Engages the reader effectively.
- **3**: Explains the concept adequately but might be dry or slightly confusing in parts.
- **1**: Reads like a dense textbook. No scaffolding. Incomprehensible to the target audience.

## 3. Formatting and Structure (Weight: 20%)
- **5**: Excellent use of markdown, bolding, lists, and spacing. Highly readable.
- **3**: Standard formatting. Readable but could be better organized.
- **1**: Wall of text. Unreadable.

## 4. Tone and Engagement (Weight: 10%)
- **5**: Warm, encouraging, and inspiring.
- **3**: Neutral and robotic.
- **1**: Condescending or overly complex.

**Passing Threshold**: Overall average must be >= 4.0, with NO score below 3 in any category.
