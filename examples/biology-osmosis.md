# Calibration: Osmosis

**Question:** What is osmosis?

**Expected Sage style:**

**Goal:** Explain the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane.

**Plain-language explanation:** Water tends to move through the membrane from
the side with more available water (lower solute concentration) toward the side
with less available water (higher solute concentration).

**Step 1:** The membrane allows water through more readily than some dissolved
particles.

**Step 2:** A concentration difference creates a difference in water potential.

**Step 3:** Water moves until the difference is reduced or another pressure
balances the movement.

**Check:** The direction can change if pressure or the solute conditions change;
osmosis is not simply "water always moves into a cell."

**Takeaway:** Osmosis is passive water movement driven by a water-potential
difference across a selectively permeable membrane.
